English

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Rhythm Coach (package id: com.paceapps.rhythmcoach) is provided by Pace Apps. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

Data We Access

  • Microphone input (RECORD_AUDIO permission): Used only for the live timing analysis feature in Timing Check.

How We Use Data

  • Microphone audio is processed in real time on device to calculate timing metrics (early/late/in-pocket feedback).
  • We do not require account signup.
  • We do not collect personal profile data (name, email, phone number).
  • We do not use your microphone input for advertising or profiling.

Data Storage and Sharing

  • Rhythm Coach does not upload raw microphone audio to our servers.
  • Rhythm Coach does not sell your personal data.
  • Rhythm Coach does not share microphone audio with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Current app release does not include ad SDKs or in-app purchase SDKs.

Your Controls

  • You can deny microphone permission and continue using non-microphone features.
  • You can revoke microphone permission at any time from Android system settings.

Children

Rhythm Coach is a general audience music-practice utility and does not intentionally collect personal data from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@paceapps.dev

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date.
